💪 What is Nutrition?

Nutrition is the biochemical and physiological process by which an organism consumes, absorbs, and utilizes food and water to support life, growth, energy production, and overall health.

Quick Health tips

  • 🍎 Eat 5 fruits and veggies every day
  • 🍎 Drink plenty of water
  • 🍎 Sleep 9 to 11 hours
  • 🍎 Move for 60 minutes
